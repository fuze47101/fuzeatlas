**Round 2 — application guidance**

Same 2.0 mg/kg on both fabrics, FUZE alone in the bath, with these settings:

| | CO twill | PES jersey |
|---|---|---|
| Make-up water | **Deionised** | **Deionised** |

| Pad | As Round 1 (please log the measured pick-up) | As Round 1 |
| Dry | Normal | Normal |
| **Fixation** | 150 °C, 60 s | **160–170 °C, 60–90 s** (below your heat-set) |
| Pre-wash ICP sample | **After one DI rinse** | After one DI rinse |

Two notes on why:

- **No pH adjustment on CO and PES.** FUZE deposits best from a neutral bath on these two fibres. The acid step is for polyamide only — where the amine end-groups need to be protonated to pull the particle onto the fibre — so for this pair we leave it out entirely.
- **The fixation pass.** On polyester, taking the fibre surface briefly above its softening point lets the particle seat into the surface rather than sit on it, and that is what carries it through a demanding wash series. Your heat-set conditions are higher than this, so it's a mild step for the line. On cotton there's no thermoplastic to soften, so the pass is a short, hot dry that maximises contact.

If you have fabric to spare, running the PES **with and without** the fixation from the same bath would give us the cleanest possible comparison — one variable, one answer.

**Three confirmations that help us reconcile the Round 1 numbers while we re-run your samples**

1. Which acid was used to bring the Round 1 bath to pH 5.5? (We only ever use acetic acid with FUZE — any chloride-bearing acid converts the silver to insoluble silver chloride, which pads on but rinses straight off. Not an issue for Round 2, since there's no acid, but it tells us how to read Round 1.)
2. Was the Round 1 bath made up with deionised or softened/tap water?
3. On the post-wash ICP digests — was HCl held at ≥10% through to analysis and the filtration done on PTFE, per Section 5 of FTP-100? Silver chloride drops out of a diluted aqua regia digest and gets filtered off, which reads as a clean, repeatable low result when the silver is in fact still on the fabric. Three of your four post-wash values landing at 0.10–0.11 is the pattern that makes me want to double-check it, on both our sides.

Also, if you can name the detergent used for the 4N cycles I'll match it on our side; ECE-A (no perborate, no brightener) is our default.
