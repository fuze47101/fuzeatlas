Dry-Fog Atomizer vs. Typical Electrostatic Sprayer

Same job. One leaves the surface bone dry. The other still wets it.

Our Unit Typical Electrostatic Sprayer
Droplet size Under 5 microns — true dry fog. Coats every surface evenly and flashes off without wetting. 40–160 microns — 10–30× larger. E-Mist EPIX360 = 75µm; EvaClean Protexus = 40/60/80µm; Ryobi = 50/75/100µm.
Surface result Stays dry — no pooling, no streaks, no wipe-down. Charged droplets wrap the surface but still leave it wet — visible deposit, needs dwell/dry time.
Power 20V Dewalt-style battery, with adapter for 18V Milwaukee M18 packs. Proprietary pack (e.g. 16.8V Protexus, 12V Electro-Mist).
Runtime 8 hours on a single 2Ah pack (~5W draw). All-day cordless. ~4 hours typical (EvaClean Protexus, 16.8V).
Battery flexibility Runs the battery platforms you already own. Locked to the maker's proprietary battery.
Coverage mechanism Particle size alone carries the fog into every gap — no charge needed. Relies on electrostatic charge to pull oversized droplets onto the surface.

Sub-5-micron output is why the surface stays dry: droplets that small carry far less liquid per unit area and evaporate on contact — full, even coverage without ever wetting the material. Electrostatic sprayers charge their droplets to improve wrap-around, but at 40–160µm those droplets are still 10–30× larger, so they deposit visible liquid and need dry time.
