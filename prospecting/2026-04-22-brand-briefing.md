# FUZE Daily Brand Briefing — April 22, 2026

**Category Focus:** Footwear / Sneakers / Work Boots / Slippers / Insoles
**Brands Researched:** 23

**Rotation note:** Apr 9 covered Athletic/Activewear; Apr 15 covered Workwear/Healthcare; Apr 20 covered Outdoor/Adventure; Apr 21 covered Hospitality/Bedding. Today rotates to **Footwear** — the single largest skin-contact textile surface we haven't addressed yet. Global athletic footwear is a ~$160B market; the work-boot/safety-shoe segment is ~$17B and growing on OSHA + ESG pressure; performance insoles alone are ~$4.5B. Three antimicrobial incumbents own the footwear market: **Agion** (silver-zeolite, owned by Sciessent, inside every Superfeet insole + Brooks running models + dozens of work-boot SKUs), **Polygiene StayFresh** (silver-chloride, inside Hoka trail models, Icebug, and Dr. Scholl's), and **Microban** (zinc pyrithione + silver variants, OEM'd across Timberland PRO, Red Wing, KEEN work lines). Every single one of these is a direct FUZE displacement target — the incumbents rely on binder-bonded silver halides or zinc chemistries that leach over home laundering, fail PFAS-adjacent scrutiny (Ca SB 707, NY Assembly A3279, EU REACH Annex XVII), and require cure ovens that footwear assembly lines don't want to add. FUZE is 99.998% DI water + 20 ppm metamaterial, bonds to linings during standard textile finishing upstream of the shoe factory, holds through 100 washes (F1), zero binders, zero cure, zero PFAS. Today's list mixes (1) the performance running houses where antimicrobial lining is table stakes, (2) the sustainability-first DTC challengers who need a clean-chemistry story, (3) the work-boot portfolio owners whose OSHA + bluesign teams can pull the supplier's hand, and (4) one pure-play insole specialist (Superfeet) whose entire product line is branded on Agion — the single highest-leverage displacement target on the list.
