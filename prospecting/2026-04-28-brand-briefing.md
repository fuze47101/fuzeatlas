Today rotates to **Military / Tactical Gear**, the highest-leverage industrial vertical we have not yet hit. Three macro tailwinds make this week the right moment: (1) **Berry Amendment + Buy American** procurement increasingly favors domestic chemistry — FUZE is Salt Lake City-manufactured, and the active is liquid-laser-ablated from recycled domestic electronics, which is a rare win for "Made in USA, sustainable, antimicrobial" all in one spec line. (2) **DoD + GSA PFAS phase-out pressure** has made every tactical brand's R&D org PFAS-screening their entire chemistry stack — they are actively looking for new antimicrobial chemistries that pass 8(a)(7) and don't carry PFAS off-balance-sheet risk. France's January 2026 PFAS textile ban (military exempt for now, expanding 2030) plus California SB 707 mean even US tactical brands selling to allies have to PFAS-clean. (3) Every legacy AM treatment in this category — including the antimicrobial baked into Drifire FR base layers — uses silver-based chemistry that the new generation of soldier-facing brand managers (Born Primitive, Viktos, Triple Aught Design) avoid in marketing because "silver/nano" reads poorly to the Gen Z enlistee. FUZE is the only PFAS-free, federal+California-EPA-registered, OEKO-TEX Class I, bluesign®-approved metamaterial that lands clean on every one of those screens. Today's list is structured in five clusters: (1) the big-five US tactical apparel houses that hold most of the law-enforcement and DoD contract volume (5.11, Propper, Tru-Spec, Vertx, Massif), (2) the premium / SOCOM-facing brands where chemistry sophistication wins specs (Crye Precision, First Spear, Beyond Clothing, Otte Gear, Wild Things Tactical), (3) the FR base-layer specialists where Drifire's antimicrobial-silver story is the direct displacement target (Drifire/National Safety Apparel, Massif base-layer line, XGO/Longworth), (4) the soldier-direct DTC veterans-owned tactical houses where ESG and PFAS-free are increasingly headline messaging (Viktos, Triple Aught Design, Born Primitive Tactical, Combat Iron, Tactical Distributors, Grunt Style), and (5) the European tactical exports already PFAS-pressured by EU regulation and shopping for compliant chemistry (UF PRO, Helikon-Tex, Carinthia, Garmont).
