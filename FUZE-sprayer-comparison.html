Dry-Fog Application System

One FUZE dry-fog pass beats spraying wet disinfectant every cycle.

A hardware advantage (sub-5-micron fog) and a chemistry advantage (durable, bonded, non-leaching) in one system.

The Hardware — Dry Fog vs. Electrostatic Sprayer

FUZE Dry-Fog System Typical Electrostatic Sprayer
Droplet size Under 5 microns — true dry fog; coats evenly and flashes off without wetting. 40–160 microns — 10–30× larger. E-Mist EPIX360 = 75µm; EvaClean Protexus = 40/60/80µm; Ryobi = 50/75/100µm.
Surface result Stays dry — no pooling, streaks, or wipe-down. Charged droplets wrap the surface but still leave it wet — visible deposit, needs dwell/dry time.
Power 20V Dewalt-style battery, adapter for 18V Milwaukee M18 packs. Proprietary pack (e.g. 16.8V Protexus, 12V Electro-Mist).
Runtime 8 hours on a single 2Ah pack (~5W draw). All-day cordless. ~4 hours typical (EvaClean Protexus, 16.8V).
Battery flexibility Runs the platforms you already own. Locked to the maker's proprietary battery.
Coverage mechanism Particle size alone carries fog into every gap — no charge needed. Relies on electrostatic charge to pull oversized droplets onto the surface.

The Chemistry — FUZE Metamaterial vs. Sprayed Disinfectants

FUZE Metamaterial Quats / Bleach / Peroxide / Alcohol
How it works Non-leaching contact-kill. Metamaterial bonds permanently to the surface and dismantles microbes on contact. Wet chemical kill — active only while the surface stays wet; works by leaching ions / oxidizing.
Durability Durable, bonded protection that keeps working between cleanings. Evaporates — protection ends when the surface dries. No meaningful residual.
Re-application Apply once — long service life per treatment. Re-spray every cleaning cycle, often multiple times a day.
Dwell time No wet dwell required — always on. Quats/bleach need a wet contact/dwell window (often up to ~10 min) to be effective.
Resistance risk Physical kill mechanism — no resistance pathway. Quaternary ammonium tolerance/resistance is documented in the literature.
Chemistry & safety PFAS-free, no binders, no harsh chemistry, no corrosive residue. Quats & bleach are skin/respiratory irritants and asthma triggers; bleach corrodes; aquatic toxicity.
Surface impact Gentle on substrates; no discoloration or corrosion. Bleach corrodes metal/grout; repeated quats build sticky film.
Validation Third-party tested — ASTM E2149, AATCC 100, ISO 20743; OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I, bluesign® approved, EPA registered. Performance ends at dry-down; no durable, test-backed residual claim.

The combined advantage: the FUZE dry-fog system lays down a sub-5-micron, even coat of a durable, non-leaching antimicrobial that bonds to the surface — so instead of re-spraying wet quat or bleach every cleaning cycle (toxic, corrosive, gone at dry-down), you treat once and stay protected between cleanings.
