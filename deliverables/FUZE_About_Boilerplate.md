## Standard boilerplate (use this version 95% of the time)

**About FUZE® Technologies**

FUZE® Technologies is the antimicrobial textile platform behind FUZE® — a non-leaching treatment that bonds proprietary metamaterial permanently into fiber surfaces during standard finishing. Unlike surface coatings or leaching biocides, FUZE® does not wash out, does not migrate to skin, and requires no PFAS, binders, or chemical curing. It works through direct contact-kill rather than ion release, which is why performance holds across commercial wash cycles instead of fading after the first dozen.

FUZE® is OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 Class I certified for direct skin contact (including infants), bluesign® approved, EPA-registered (federal), California EPA approved, and PFAS-free. Performance is validated by independent laboratories against AATCC 100, ASTM E2149, AATCC 30 (antifungal), and ISO 18184 (antiviral). The treatment is offered in four tiers — F1 Full Spectrum, F2 Advanced Performance, F3 Core Performance, and F4 Foundation — across hospitality, healthcare, activewear, and home textiles.

FUZE® is manufactured at the company's solar-capable facility in Salt Lake City, Utah using recycled-electronics feedstock and applied through standard textile finishing equipment (exhaust, pad-dry-cure, or spray). FUZE® Technologies' core platform also extends to thermal management and IR-deflection applications currently in development with leading performance brands. Learn more at **fuze47.com**.

**Media contact:** Andrew Peterson, CEO and Founder · andrew@fuze47.com

## Long version (~250 words — for white papers or feature-length editorial)

**About FUZE® Technologies**

FUZE® Technologies is the antimicrobial textile platform behind FUZE® — a non-leaching treatment that bonds proprietary metamaterial permanently into fiber surfaces during standard finishing. Unlike surface coatings, leaching biocides, or silver-ion chemistries, FUZE® does not wash out, does not migrate to skin, and requires no PFAS, binders, or chemical curing. The treatment works through direct contact-kill rather than ion release, which is why performance holds across commercial wash cycles instead of degrading after the first dozen washes.

FUZE® is OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 Class I certified for direct skin contact (including infants), bluesign® approved, EPA-registered (federal), California EPA approved, and PFAS-free. Performance is validated by independent laboratories against AATCC 100, ASTM E2149 (the dynamic-contact test designed for non-leaching antimicrobials), AATCC 30 (antifungal), and ISO 18184 (antiviral). FUZE® is offered in four performance tiers — F1 Full Spectrum (rated through 100 commercial washes), F2 Advanced Performance (75), F3 Core Performance (50), and F4 Foundation (25) — across hospitality, healthcare, activewear, performance apparel, and home textiles.

FUZE® is manufactured at the company's solar-capable facility in Salt Lake City, Utah using recycled-electronics feedstock as the source material, then delivered as a water-based finishing liquid applied on standard textile finishing equipment (exhaust dyebath, pad-dry-cure, or spray) with no special equipment, no curing oven, and no shelf-life limit. FUZE® Technologies' core platform also extends to thermal management and IR-deflection applications currently in development with leading performance brands.

Learn more at **fuze47.com**. Media inquiries: Andrew Peterson, CEO and Founder · andrew@fuze47.com.

## What this boilerplate intentionally does NOT say

- **Never say "silver," "nano," "nanoparticle," "silver-ion," "Ag," or any variant** — even though chemically that's what the active material is. The Critical Brand Language table in `CLAUDE.md` is non-negotiable. Compliance docs (CIL, ARSL, SDS) may name the chemistry; marketing and press boilerplate may not.
- **Never claim "EPA-validated wash count"** — EPA registers chemistry as a pesticide; it does not certify wash counts. Wash counts are FUZE-internal AATCC 100 / ISO 20743 testing with third-party reports available on request.
- **Never name specific brand customers** (PVH, KUIU, Penfabric, Welspun, Nike, North Face, etc.) in the boilerplate. Customer name-drops are a separate decision per release and require the customer's PR sign-off. If a customer wants to be named in their own release, that's their call to make in their own copy — but the FUZE About block stays customer-neutral.
- **Never say "patent-pending" or "patented"** in boilerplate unless Andrew confirms the patent status of the specific tier being discussed. Generic boilerplate stays out of IP claims.
