The opportunity

The antimicrobial textile market (~$14B) is being reshaped by a regulatory and brand-led retreat from legacy chemistry — silver-ion, zinc, quats and PFAS-bound finishes that leach into skin and wastewater and face tightening biocide scrutiny (EU BPR, California SB 707, the Texas-AG/Lululemon PFAS actions). Brands need a finish that performs and survives that scrutiny. FUZE is that finish — and because the same metamaterial bonds to hard surfaces, antimicrobial textiles are the entry point to a ~$49B surface platform (plastics, coatings, hard-surface sanitization).

Why FUZE wins — the moat

01 · Safety & zero toxicity
Non-leaching by design — bonds into the fiber and kills on contact. OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I (baby-safe), bluesign® approved, EPA registered (federal) + California EPA (Q1 2026), PFAS-free. A finish you can put on a newborn's onesie.
02 · Sustainability
Active at 0.25–1.0 mg/kg. No PFAS, binders, or curing burden. Metamaterial is laser-synthesized from recycled electronics — circular feedstock, low-energy, solar-capable production. Drops into existing finishing lines (exhaust, pad-dry-cure, spray) with no capex.
03 · Unmatched IP & supply
58 worldwide patents across the material, its synthesis, and its application. The only producer on earth via proprietary liquid laser ablation. Competitors can copy a claim; they cannot make the molecule. The moat is the material.
04 · Performance platform
One treatment, a full stack: antimicrobial, anti-odor, antifungal, antiviral, UV protection, moisture-wicking and IR/radiant-heat management — all durable to 100 washes. Validated on ASTM E2149, the test built for non-leaching contact-kill.
