- Added the **2024 EPA federal registration** and **California EPA approval (Q1 2026)** as headline certifications, alongside OEKO-TEX® Class I, bluesign®, and PFAS-free.

**Brand language & product**
- Added the **metamaterial vocabulary standard** (never silver / nano / ion in marketing) and FUZE® Technologies vs. Biotech naming.
- Added the **F1–F4 product system**, application methods, and the **ASTM E2149 vs. AATCC 100** testing position.

**Identity & digital**
- New logo lockups extracted from master art (white / navy / gradient · stacked / horizontal / icon), clear-space & min-size rules, co-branding "Treated with FUZE®" 35% rule, misuse page.
- Updated all destinations to **fuze47.com**, fuzeatlas.com, fuzefaq.com.

**Structure**
- Removed the 15 pages of raw lab forms and test reports from the 2021 file. Those printed actual treatment recipes (e.g. "3ppm, 7ml/min") — a confidentiality risk in a partner-facing book — and were dated 2020. Current third-party reports now live in the Atlas partner portal / available on request.

## Update — Version 2.3 (Tier-model correction — IMPORTANT)
Corrected a core product-positioning error per Andrew: **wash durability is NOT tied to tier.** Fixes applied everywhere:
- Removed all "F1 = 100 washes / F2 = 75 / F3 = 50 / F4 = 25" framing (pages 4, 6, 13, 18).
- New tier model: **the metamaterial bonds permanently at every application level** — durability is a function of the bond and the **substrate**, not the dose. Tiers differ in **antimicrobial test performance** and the **ancillary benefit stack** (reduced water surface tension, faster drying, UV/color protection, microfiber), plus **price**.
- **F1 Full Spectrum is the flagship** and carries the full ancillary stack. **F2/F3/F4 are value-priced, not lower quality or less durable.**
- Tier cards now show **dose (mg/kg)**, not wash counts. Product page table column changed from "Wash rating" to "Ancillary benefit stack."
