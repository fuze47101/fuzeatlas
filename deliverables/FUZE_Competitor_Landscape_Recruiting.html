How to work it

Two motions, kept distinct. (1) Network with people currently in the field — warm, no-agenda connections that build a friendly presence in the industry. (2) Recruit former employees — people who've left these companies and carry the mill/brand relationships and application know-how, for FUZE contract/commission sales (same model as our existing BD reps). We target former employees for recruiting — not active poaching of current staff.

Template A · Friendly LinkedIn connection (current industry people)
"Hi [First] — I'm on the FUZE side of antimicrobial textiles and have followed [Company]'s work in the space. Always glad to connect with people who know this corner of the industry well — no agenda, happy to trade notes anytime. — Andrew, FUZE Biotech"
Template B · Recruiting a former employee (contract / commission sales)
"Hi [First] — your antimicrobial-textile background at [Company] is exactly the field experience that's hard to find. I'm Andrew, founder of FUZE Biotech — we make the only non-leaching antimicrobial metamaterial (sole-source, 58 patents, already in testing with Nike, Patagonia, Spanx, SanMar and others). We're expanding our field network and work with experienced reps on a contract / commission basis — you'd sell into mills and brands you already know. Worth a 15-minute call to see if it fits? Either way, glad to connect."

Finding the people (no paid tools)

Use a Google X-ray search to surface profiles — it catches both current and former employees because profiles list past roles. Example for Sciessent:

site:linkedin.com/in ("Sciessent" OR "Agion") ("technical sales" OR "business development" OR "technical service" OR application OR "account manager") (textile OR apparel OR antimicrobial)

For former employees only, use LinkedIn People search and set the "Past company" filter (leave "Current company" blank). The companion spreadsheet has a ready X-ray string for all eight companies plus an outreach tracker.

Bottom line: Microban (+ Ultra-Fresh), LANXESS/Silvadur, and Sciessent hold the deepest US-based antimicrobial-textile field talent. Build friendly industry relationships broadly; recruit their alumni — who already own the mill and brand relationships — as FUZE contract reps. The spreadsheet is your working list.
