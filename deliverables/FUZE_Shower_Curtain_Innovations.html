FUZE-Protected Shower Curtains

Fights mold. Dries faster. Stays fresh. Lasts longer.

FUZE is a proprietary, permanently-bonded metamaterial finish engineered into the curtain — not sprayed on. It keeps the curtain cleaner and fresher in the one place textiles struggle most: the constantly-damp bathroom. Clean, non-leaching, PFAS-free chemistry that nothing rinses out of.

5 Innovations built into every FUZE curtain

01
Mold & Mildew Defense
Inhibits the growth of mold and mildew on the curtain — the #1 cause of pink/black staining, musty odor, and early replacement. Stays visibly cleaner, far longer.
02
All-Day Odor Control
Inhibits odor-causing bacteria so the curtain resists the musty, wet-bathroom smell between washes — fresher feel, fewer launderings.
03
Quick-Dry Performance
An engineered surface sheds moisture and dries faster after every shower — removing the standing dampness that lets mildew take hold in the first place.
04
Built to Last — 100+ Washes
Protection is permanently bonded into the fiber and durable through 100+ launderings. The curtain stays protected for its full life — extending replacement cycles and adding real consumer value.
05
Clean by Design — Non-Leaching & PFAS-Free
FUZE bonds to the fiber and works by contact — it does not leach. Nothing rinses into the bath water, down the drain, or onto skin. PFAS-free, formaldehyde-free, no binders, and certified safe for babies and sensitive skin (OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I). The clean alternative to leaching antimicrobial finishes.
