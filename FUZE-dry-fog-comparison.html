The Chemistry — FUZE Metamaterial vs. Sprayed Disinfectants

FUZE MetamaterialQuats / Bleach / Peroxide / Alcohol
How it works Non-leaching contact-kill. Metamaterial bonds permanently to the surface and dismantles microbes on contact. Wet chemical kill — active only while the surface stays wet; works by leaching ions / oxidizing.
Durability Durable, bonded protection that keeps working between cleanings. Evaporates — protection ends when the surface dries. No meaningful residual.
Re-application Apply once — long service life per treatment. Re-spray every cleaning cycle, often multiple times a day.
Dwell time No wet dwell required — always on. Quats/bleach need a wet contact window (often up to ~10 min) to be effective.
Resistance risk Physical kill mechanism — no resistance pathway. Quaternary ammonium tolerance/resistance is documented in the literature.
Corrosion Non-corrosive. Trace metamaterial (20 ppm), no chlorine or oxidizers — safe on metals, electronics, seals. Bleach pits stainless & eats aluminum; peroxide/peracetic attack brass & copper; chloride quats corrode contacts — and degrade the sprayer's own internals.
Surface feel No change to hand or finish — no film, no residue, no stiffening on textiles. Quats leave a tacky, dust-grabbing film; bleach leaves chalky residue and can dull finishes; repeated wet passes re-deposit every cycle.
Chemistry & safety PFAS-free, no binders, no harsh chemistry, no corrosive residue. Quats & bleach are skin/respiratory irritants and asthma triggers; bleach corrodes; aquatic toxicity.
Validation Third-party tested — ASTM E2149, AATCC 100, ISO 20743; OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I, bluesign® approved, EPA registered. Performance ends at dry-down; no durable, test-backed residual claim.
